2. Who can use the Exchange

  • You must be 18 or over and use your real name and contact details.

  • You're responsible for keeping your password safe and for everything done through your account.

3. How introductions work

  • Professionals appear to approved companies anonymously, under a reference number.
  • A company can ask to chat with a professional. The professional sees the company's name and decides whether to accept.
  • When a professional accepts, that is an introduction made by Consumer Talent. It is recorded on the protection record and stays protected for 12 months from the latest interaction.
  • A professional's name and contact details are only shared once they've accepted and the company has signed our terms.

4. For professionals

  • The Exchange is free for professionals. We never charge you a fee.
  • Keep your profile accurate and up to date, and tell us if you've already interviewed with a company when it asks to chat.
  • Once you've accepted an introduction, please keep conversations with that company on the Exchange, or let us know if you talk directly, so the introduction stays on record.

5. For hiring companies

  • An introduction fee is due if you hire, or engage in any way, a professional introduced through the Exchange within the protection period, including where the professional contacts you directly or you find them another way. The full fee terms are in the Client Terms of Business.
  • Use professionals' information only to consider them for roles at your company. Never pass it to anyone outside your business.
  • Tell us promptly when an introduced professional accepts an offer.

6. Things you mustn't do

  • Share contact details, email addresses, phone numbers or links in chat before an introduction has been made. The Exchange blocks these, and we'll be told.
  • Try to identify an anonymous professional, or contact them outside the Exchange, before they've accepted an introduction.
  • Copy, scrape or download profiles, or use the Exchange to build a list of candidates or companies.
  • Post anything false, misleading, offensive or unlawful, or pretend to be someone else.
  • Interfere with the Exchange's security or try to access accounts or data that aren't yours.

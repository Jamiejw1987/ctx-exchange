How it works

Introductions, not job boards

Every introduction goes through Consumer Talent, so both sides only meet people worth meeting.

1

Profiles stay anonymous

Professionals share their experience, wins and what they want next, without their name or employer. Companies share who they are and the roles they're hiring.

2

We make the introduction

A company asks to meet someone. We review every request, then pass it on. The professional decides whether to accept.

3

Talk, reveal, hire

Chat in confidence, reveal identities only when you're both ready, then book interviews and agree an offer, all in one place.

For hiring teams

Meet senior talent your competitors can't reach

The best people aren't on job boards. They're quietly open to the right move, and they'll only show their hand somewhere private.

What you can do

  • Browse a vetted pool of senior food and drink professionals, anonymously
  • Get hand-picked matches for every open role, with a note on why we've put them forward
  • Shortlist, request introductions and chat, all in the app
  • Invite colleagues from any department, all covered by one agreement
  • Reveal identities, book interviews and make offers once both sides agree
  • Pay a fee only when you hire

What you can't do

  • See names, employers or contact details before the professional agrees
  • Contact professionals outside the Exchange
  • See your own employees, and they can't see you
